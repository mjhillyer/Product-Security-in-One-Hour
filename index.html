Security is part of patient safety

Connected medical devices can be attacked, and an attack on a device can hurt a patient, sometimes many patients at once. Regulators now expect security to be designed in and maintained for a device's whole life. This course gives everyone on the team the shared foundation to do that, using four fictional devices as worked examples.

Why devices are different

Patient harm, multi-patient harm, long service lives, and hospitals and homes the manufacturer doesn't control.

Safety and security

How a security weakness becomes a hazardous situation, and why integrity and availability of therapy come first.

Secure by Design

The principles behind good design decisions, threat modeling in brief, the SBOM, and the trade-offs unique to devices.

The rules, worldwide

FDA and Section 524B, the EU MDR, other major markets, IMDRF, and the standards that tie them together.

Before and after release

The evidence regulators expect, vulnerability handling and disclosure, patching, and planning for end of support.

Your role

Who does what, when to bring in product security, and what to do if you suspect a vulnerability.

The hour, module by module

Each module is short, ends with a hands-on “Try it” exercise and a worked example, and links to a printable guide. There are no quizzes and nothing to sign up for.
